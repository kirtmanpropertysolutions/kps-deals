About the Tournament

Where Golf Meets Purpose

The Lake Chelan Memory Cup is an annual charity golf tournament held each summer on the stunning shores of Lake Chelan, Washington. Now in its 9th year, the event brings together golfers, sponsors, and the community in a shared mission to fight Alzheimer's disease.

Set against the breathtaking backdrop of Bear Mountain Ranch, the tournament combines world-class golf with incredible hospitality — featuring a two-day scramble, evening dinners, auction items, and unforgettable moments.

Every dollar raised goes directly to the Alzheimer's Association, funding research and care support for the millions of families affected by this disease.

Why We Play

In His Honor

The Lake Chelan Memory Cup was founded by David Kirtman to honor his father — a man whose influence left a mark on every life he touched. In his career, his community, and the world of sports, he was a pioneer. He led by example, lifted others without being asked, and carried himself with the kind of grace that people spend a lifetime trying to find.

When Alzheimer's began to take hold, those who loved him wanted to respond in a way that matched the scale of who he was. So they built something. Nine years later, that something has become a tradition — a weekend on the water where family, friends, and the community gather not just to golf, but to celebrate a legacy that refuses to be forgotten.

Event Information

What to Expect

⛳

Scramble Format

Teams of four compete in a classic scramble format across the pristine fairways of Bear Mountain Ranch. All skill levels are welcome — this is a tournament about fun, camaraderie, and giving back.

🍷

Evening Dinners

Both evenings feature hosted dinners showcasing the finest wines and cuisine from the Lake Chelan wine region, bringing players and sponsors together in celebration of community.

🏆

Awards & Auction

A live awards ceremony crowns the champions, while a silent auction and sponsorship recognition spotlight the generous individuals and businesses making it all possible.

📍

Bear Mountain Ranch

Nestled above Lake Chelan, Bear Mountain Ranch offers dramatic views, an exceptional course, and resort-style amenities — setting the stage for an unforgettable weekend.

🤝

Sponsorship Opportunities

Align your brand with a meaningful cause. Multiple sponsorship tiers are available — from hole sponsorships to title naming rights. Get in touch to learn more.

🎗️

Benefiting Alzheimer's Research

Proceeds benefit the Alzheimer's Association — the leading voluntary health organization in Alzheimer's care, support, and research. Every round played makes a difference.
